## Key Findings

### Current SC NP Practice Authority Status

**South Carolina is a RESTRICTED PRACTICE STATE** as of February 2026.

#### Requirements:
1. **Collaborative Practice Agreement Required**
   - NPs MUST have a practice agreement with a physician to practice
   - Physician must be located within South Carolina borders
   - If physician leaves the state (conference, vacation) and no alternate is listed, NP cannot practice
   - One physician can only supervise maximum of 6 full-time equivalent NPs

2. **Prescriptive Authority**
   - Requires physician collaboration agreement
   - Cannot prescribe independently without this agreement

3. **Pending Legislation (2026)**
   - Bill SB45/HB3580 proposed to grant full practice authority
   - Would remove collaborative agreement requirement for APRNs with 2,000+ clinical hours
   - Currently under consideration, not yet passed

## Home Visit Regulations

### Critical Finding:
**No specific prohibition against NPs providing care in patient homes** under current SC law, PROVIDED:

1. ✅ NP has valid collaborative practice agreement with SC physician
2. ✅ Services fall within NP scope of practice
3. ✅ NP maintains appropriate licensure and credentials

### Home vs. Mobile Clinic (Sprinter Van)

**IMPORTANT DISTINCTION:**

- **Individual Home Visits:** Likely permissible under NP license with collaborative agreement
- **Mobile Clinic/Van:** May require additional licensing as a "mobile health unit" or "ambulatory care facility"
- **Recommendation:** Consult SC Department of Health and Environmental Control (DHEC) for mobile clinic requirements

**SAFEST APPROACH for Andrew:**
- ✅ Home visits to patient residences (parties of 3+) = ALLOWED under NP collaborative practice
- ⚠️ Sprinter van clinic = May require additional facility licensing - needs verification

2. **Specialty procedures not requiring specialist**
