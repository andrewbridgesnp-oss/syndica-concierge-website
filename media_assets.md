## Design Specifications

### Color Palette
- **Background:** Silver/White (#F5F5F5, #FFFFFF)
- **Primary Text:** Black (#000000, #1A1A1A)
- **Accent:** Electric Pink (#FF1F8F)
- **Secondary:** Light Gray (#E0E0E0)
- **Borders:** Silver (#C0C0C0)

### Typography
- **Headings:** Outfit (Bold, 600-700 weight)
- **Body:** Inter or System UI (400-500 weight)
- **Accent Text:** Pink highlights on key terms

### Design Style
- **Theme:** Professional Medical with Modern Edge
- **Approach:** Clean, bright, trustworthy
- **Contrast:** High contrast for accessibility
- **Imagery:** Hyper-realistic professional photography
- **NO EMOJIS:** Text and icons only
